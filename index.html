CHAPTER ONE

The day everything
became a memory.

I thought the hardest part would be saying goodbye.

I was wrong.

Sometimes a person disappears from your everyday life, but somehow remains in every ordinary thing—the songs you skip, the places you avoid, the messages you never send.

This is not only a story about someone who disappeared. It is about the life that continued after she did.
